# Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [한국어](docs/ko/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [简体中文](docs/zh-CN/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our commitment

TERMCADE contributors, maintainers, and community members are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free environment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, socioeconomic status, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, never the person presenting them.
- Accept feedback gracefully and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing communication styles.
- Prefer language that is inclusive and understandable to an international community.
- Put community safety and project health ahead of winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Impersonation, spam, manipulation, or knowingly misleading claims.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

These rules apply in repository activity, project communication, events, and public spaces when someone represents TERMCADE.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/ium-mui/TERMCADE/security/advisories/new). Put `Code of Conduct` in the advisory title. Do not include more personal information than necessary.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report when practical, protect the reporter's privacy, hear relevant parties, document the decision privately, and disclose only what is necessary for community safety. Maintainers with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves.

Enforcement may include:

1. **Correction:** a private explanation and request to change behavior.
2. **Warning:** a formal warning with consequences for repetition.
3. **Temporary restriction:** limited participation or contact for a defined period.
4. **Permanent ban:** removal from project spaces for severe or repeated violations.

Knowingly false or bad-faith reports may themselves violate this policy. A report that cannot be substantiated is not automatically considered bad faith.

## Scope and attribution

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy fairly. This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and common open-source enforcement practices.
